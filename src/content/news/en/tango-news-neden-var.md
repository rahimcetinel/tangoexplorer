Tango news is scattered. The festival calendar sits on one site, the city milonga on another guide, a last-minute cancellation in an Instagram story. Tango News tries to gather that scatter into one vitrine, as short stories.

The model is a club news desk: show the source, do not steal the text, send the reader to the original page. English is the default language; Turkish is the other edition.

There is no automatic pull from social accounts yet. To get an announcement onto the site, drop text into `sources/inbox/`. When the agent runs once a day, it writes a short story.
