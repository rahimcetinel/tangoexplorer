Mesopotamia Tango Marathon takes place in Diyarbakır on 16–20 September 2026. Tangocat lists the event under Turkey.

Five days is enough for a city milonga run and a short tango trip for first-time visitors. Hall, teachers, and DJs update on the source site; this story is only a date and city summary.

Tango marathons in the southeast are still uncommon. For anyone dancing off the Istanbul–Antalya line, these dates are worth marking.
