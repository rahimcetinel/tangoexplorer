Fethiye Tango Camp Vol 8 runs 17–21 September 2026 in Fethiye. Tangocat marks it as a camp: a slower mix of classes and seaside days than a marathon floor.

September in Fethiye still offers open air and late-summer light. The format suits people who want workshops and several days of dancing more than a milonga-only weekend.

Registration, lodging notes, and the current faculty are on the source page. This text announces the event and its dates; it is not a copy of the programme.
